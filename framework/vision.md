## The vision, in a few lines
An independent professional **training and certification organisation** for Data & AI, built on **expert-led delivery** — face-to-face, live online and corporate/private — with a portal that *supports* that ecosystem: public course pages, scheduled offerings, registration and payment, participant accounts, reviews, a verifiable **Certificate of Completion**, and administrator and trainer operations (`DR-02`).

Beside it, a second product line: **free, self-paced learning** from the founder's own book *I Am Datapedia!* and a free **Assessment Check** whose result carries a verifiable ID (`DR-03`, `DR-05`, `DR-06`, `DR-07`).

## Principles that do not move
- **One earned credential** — the Certificate of Completion (`DR-01`); free assessment results are never that credential.
- Evidence-based, verifiable certificates.
- Experts, not content volume, are the product.
