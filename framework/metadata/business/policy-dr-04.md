## Purpose
Records the permission to call the free Knowledge Check product line's page and menu item "Free Certifications".

## Description
- **Decision.** "Free Certifications" may be a page title and header/footer menu label for the free Knowledge Check product line (alongside "Free Trainings" for the free reading line, together replacing "Free Training & Certification"). It is a naming permission for the product line, not for any individual result: results stay described as a "Knowledge Check result", never "a certificate" or "a certification"; the disclaimer that a result is not the Academy's credential keeps appearing; results are not added to the credential-integrity policy; the account area keeps them visibly separate.
- **Date approved.** 2026-09-28 (founder: "Free Certifications, and amend DR-01/DR-03").
- **Amends.** DR-03 §3 naming rule, narrowly. DR-01 unchanged.
- **Superseded by.** **DR-07 (2026-10-01) supersedes DR-04**: the page and menu are now "Assessment". DR-05 §2.2 also narrowed DR-04 §2.2 for passed results.
- **Plain words.** Historical record of when the page name briefly carried "Certifications" while results themselves were still not called certificates.
