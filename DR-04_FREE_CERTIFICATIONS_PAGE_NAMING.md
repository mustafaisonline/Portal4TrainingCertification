# DECISION RECORD DR-04 — "FREE CERTIFICATIONS" AS A PAGE NAME

**Status:** Approved by the founder, 2026-09-28 (chat: *"New more change"*, item 2 — asked to split "Free Training & Certification" into two pages, "Free Trainings" and "Free Certifications"; when told this collided with DR-01/DR-03's naming rule and asked to choose, the founder answered *"Free Certifications, and amend DR-01/DR-03"*). Amends DR-03 §3's naming rule — **narrowly, as stated in §2 below**. DR-01 is **unchanged** (§3).

## 1. Why

DR-03 §3 says a Knowledge Check result "is never called a certificate of the Academy's credential." That rule was written to stop a free, unproctored self-test from being mistaken for the Academy's one earned credential (DR-01). The founder now wants the free product line's own page and header/footer menu item to be called **"Free Certifications"** — a product-line label, not a claim about any one result. Read literally, DR-03's wording would forbid naming the page that, so the founder was asked directly and chose to keep the name and amend the rule rather than pick a different name.

## 2. The decision (amends DR-03 §3)

1. **"Free Certifications" may be used as a page title and a header/footer menu label** for the free Knowledge Check product line (alongside "Free Trainings" for the free reading/self-check product line — together replacing the single "Free Training & Certification" page and nav item).
2. This is a **naming permission for the product line as a whole**, not for any individual result. On the page itself, in the Knowledge Check flow, on the result page and on the printable result document, the wording DR-03 §3 already requires stays exactly as it is:
   - the result is described as a **"Knowledge Check result,"** never as **"a certificate"** or **"a certification"**;
   - the sentence **"A Knowledge Check result is not the Academy's credential. The Certificate of Completion is earned by attending an expert-led training"** (or equivalent) continues to appear wherever a result is shown;
   - the result is **not** added to the credential-integrity policy;
   - the account area keeps the Knowledge Check's results visibly separate from actual certificates (the existing "Your Knowledge Check results" list under Certifications, DR-03 §3, is precedent for this: an umbrella label ("Certifications") already sits over a section that plainly says the items under it are not certificates — this record extends the same pattern to the public-facing page name).
3. Every other word of DR-03 stands: free learning and the Knowledge Check remain free, self-paced, and outside the paid-training product; the Knowledge Check needs an account; nothing about eligibility, pass mark, retakes or the unlock fee changes.

## 3. What DR-01 still means (unchanged)

Nothing here creates a second credential. The Academy still issues **one credential**: the Certificate of Completion, earned by attending an expert-led training. A page called "Free Certifications" is a marketing label for a free, self-graded practice product — it is not a second path to the Academy's credential, and the product itself must go on saying so, in the words above, everywhere a result appears.

## 4. What this record does NOT decide

- The exact copy of the two new pages, their URLs, or how the header/footer menu is restructured around them — implementation, following this naming permission.
- Nothing about the physical data model changes here; no schema is affected by a page split.
