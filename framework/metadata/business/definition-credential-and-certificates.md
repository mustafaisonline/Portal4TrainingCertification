## Purpose
States what each issued document is and is not, so the Academy's one credential is never confused with a free-assessment certificate.

## Description
| Item | What it is | What it is not | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The one credential** (DR-01) | A single credential definition; no ladder, levels, bands or tiers. Always evidence-based (applied artifact graded by a human). DR-03/DR-05/DR-06 name it the **Certificate of Completion**: earned by attending an expert-led training, recorded by an administrator (DR-03 §3); the Terms say it is issued when a participant completes a training as recorded by the trainer. | Not obtainable by attendance alone under DR-02 §6 policy; not obtainable from any free product. | DR-01, DR-02, DR-03 §3 |
| **Certificate of Completion** | The Academy's one credential, as above. | Not a Knowledge Check / Assessment Check result and not a Certificate of Achievement. | DR-03 §3, DR-05 §3 |
| **Certificate of Achievement** (DR-05) | A system-generated, printable, verifiable document for a **passed** free check; valid one year from the pass date; revocable by an administrator with a recorded reason; verification page says Valid / Expired / Revoked; no signature or signatory. Viewing sits behind review plus unlock fee. | Never called a Certificate of Completion or the Academy's credential; never claims HRD Corp status; not added to the credential-integrity policy as a credential (founder review pending); a failed result never gets one. | DR-05 |
| **Graded certificates** (DR-06) | The same Certificate of Achievement carrying a grade line from a 200-question, 3-hour Free Assessment Check passed at 60 %: **Charlie** 60-70 % (120-141), **Bravo** 71-80 % (142-161), **Alpha** 81-100 % (162-200). Grade is derived from the score, never stored. Earlier results (50/100/200 questions, 70 % mark, no grade) stay as issued. | Not three credentials; not a path to the Academy's credential. | DR-06 |
| **Knowledge Check result** (DR-03) | The stored, verifiable result (ID prefix `KC-`) of the free check; a failed result keeps its ID and a "Not passed" verification page. | Not a credential. | DR-03, DR-05, DR-06 |
| **Interview Assessment result** (DR-07) | Per-role interview self-assessment: free, account required, no certificate, no pass mark. | Not a certificate. | DR-07 §4.1 |

Whether the credential-integrity policy will describe the certificates and grades is not determined from the source: the policy is an unpublished placeholder awaiting the founder (CR-2026-10-02-0627).
