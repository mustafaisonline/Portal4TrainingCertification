# UX Review — is the portal friendly enough for end users?

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Requested by:** the founder (*"treat yourself as Senior UI/UX Architecture and test the whole portal from the angle, is it friendly enough for end users?"*) · **State reviewed:** commit `d20c410` on `feat/production-foundation`, dev server `localhost:3100`, dev database (383 topics, 100 reviewed questions, two open dates).

**Status:** founder answered *"U1-U6 = yes, all recommendations accepted, commit and fix"* the same day → **fixed; see §8** (what changed, where, how it was verified).

---

## 1. Verdict

**Friendly enough to test with real people; not yet friendly enough to launch.** The bones are right: one design system, honest copy, good empty states, a checkout that says exactly what will be charged and why, a reading experience for the book that is calm and legible, dark mode that works, and every page passing the automated accessibility checks. What gets in the way is a small number of concrete defects (a header that overlaps itself on ordinary laptops, a "Finish" that mints a permanent 0 / 50 result without asking), some stale copy left behind by the fast build, and one vocabulary problem — *courses*, *trainings* and *programmes* all mean the same thing on the same page.

Fix the four defects in §3, settle the vocabulary, and the portal is ready for a friends-and-family round.

## 2. How it was tested

Three people, three journeys, in the browser, at three widths (1280 px laptop, 800 px tablet, 375 px phone), light and dark:

| Person | Journey walked |
|---|---|
| **First-time visitor, signed out** | Home → Trainings & HRD Corp → a training page → its dates → Free Training & Certification → topics → a topic and its self-check → Trainers → Reviews → header search → Verify → For Organisations → Contact → FAQ → About → Register → Sign in |
| **Participant about to pay** | Sign in → Profile → My Trainings (empty) → Certifications (empty) → Orders (empty) → Notifications → Help → Schedule → Register → the checkout page up to the Stripe button (not pressed) |
| **Learner using Free Learning** | Knowledge Check start page → a 50-question attempt → Finish → the result page and its document gate → Reviews while signed in |

Not reviewed: the admin and Trainer areas (not end users), the Stripe-hosted page, e-mails.

## 3. Defects — fix before anyone else sees it

| # | Where | What a user sees | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Header, 1280–~1400 px (most laptops) | The **"Home" link overlaps the wordmark** ("Data & AI Academy**ome**"). The five-item navigation only appears at ≥ 1280 px (`xl:`); between 1024 and 1279 px a laptop user gets the phone burger. | The first thing every visitor sees is broken on the most common screen size. | Drop the redundant "Home" link (the logo is Home), show the navigation from 1024 px with tighter spacing, narrow the search box; verify at 1024 / 1280 / 1440. `src/shared/chrome/PublicShell.tsx` |
| **D2** | Knowledge Check, any page | **"Finish and see my result" is live on page 1 with 0 of 50 answered** and goes straight to *"Not passed — 0 of 50"* with a permanent `KC-` ID. No confirmation. | One mis-click gives a person a failed result with an ID they may think is on record. | Confirm when questions are unanswered: *"12 of 50 are unanswered and will count as wrong. Finish anyway?"* `[attemptId]/AttemptForm.tsx`, `knowledge-check.actions.ts` |
| **D3** | Free Training & Certification landing | The Learn card says *"and, soon, a self-check at the end of each"* and carries a **"Self-checks coming"** chip — but self-checks are live on ten topics. | Tells the visitor a feature does not exist when it does. Left over from Phase 2. | Drive the sentence and chip from the reviewed-question count: *"Self-checks on 10 topics so far."* `app/(public)/free-learning/page.tsx` |
| **D4** | Reviews (public and signed in) | A review of Free Learning is shown under the **flagship training's title** ("Data Blueprint & AI/Vibe Coding") because the diagnostic-kind review borrows the flagship's programme row. | A visitor reads it as a review of a paid training they never attended. | Label diagnostic-kind reviews "Free Learning" in the public list and the account list. `app/(public)/reviews/page.tsx`, reviews repository read model |

## 4. Friction — worth fixing soon

**F1 · One word for the product.** The hero button says **Explore Courses**; the three-pathways card says **Explore courses**; the price cards say **Register or check course details →**; the navigation says **Trainings**; the URL is `/programs`; the sign-in page promises *"your programmes, orders and profile"*; the Certifications tab says *"when you complete the programme"*. New visitors do not know these are the same thing. Recommendation: **"training"** everywhere a user reads it (the URL can stay).

**F2 · The home page tries to do three jobs.** Above the fold: a five-line headline on a phone, six feature tiles, and promise-heavy copy (*"1–2 top candidates will be brought to Malaysia for job opportunities"*, *"Freelance Ready"*). Mid-page: the **entire ten-question diagnostic inline**, then "Three pathways", then "Your learning journey". On a phone the first screen is the headline alone. Recommendation: one message in the hero, the diagnostic as a teaser card linking to `/free-learning/diagnostic`, and the promises checked against the open public-copy findings (`PROJECT_STATUS.md` §3 item 4).

**F3 · Price cards are dense.** Each training card lists four price rows (Malaysia via HRD Corp, without HRD Corp, Pakistan, rest of the world) with strike-through "was" prices, "75 % OFF" chips and two explanatory paragraphs; on a phone that is a screen and a half per training, and the call to action at the bottom is a **text link**, not a button. Recommendation: lead with the visitor's region (profile country when signed in; otherwise Malaysia), fold the others under "Other regions", and make the action a button — **See dates and register**.

**F4 · Two buttons, one destination.** On a training page the hero offers **Register for a date** and **See upcoming dates**; both open the same filtered schedule. Keep one. The hero illustration (grey figures at a whiteboard) reads as placeholder art next to real photography elsewhere.

**F5 · HRD Corp reads as a contradiction.** The home tile says *"HRD Corp Accredited Trainer — Verified — Trainer ID 68933"*; the Trainings page says *"Becoming an HRD Corp Registered Training Provider — application in progress — not yet registered"*. Both are true (a person's accreditation vs an organisation's registration) but a visitor sees "verified" and "not yet" about the same badge. Recommendation: one sentence under the home tile: *"The trainer is accredited; the Academy's provider registration is in progress."*

**F6 · FAQ contradicts the schedule.** *"When is the next start date?"* is answered **To be confirmed** while `/schedule` lists 28 Sept and 2 Oct. The answer should be *"See the schedule"* with the link, and the chip removed.

**F7 · Knowledge Check questions assume a chapter.** Stems such as *"In the chapter's example, which column is declared the PRIMARY key?"* and *"By what other names does the chapter say Entity Recognition is known?"* make sense under a topic page but not in a 50-question mix drawn from many topics — the topic label above each question helps, but the stem should stand alone. Recommendation: an editorial rule for every batch (*no "the chapter"; name the subject*) and a pass over the 100 already reviewed.

**F8 · The failed result still sells the document.** *"Not passed — 0 of 50"* is followed at once by the US$10 unlock offer. It is honest (the document records the fail) but it lands badly. Founder's call (§6).

**F9 · Verify page mentions only certificate IDs.** The box accepts a `KC-` Knowledge Check ID too, and the header search says so; the Verify page should as well.

**F10 · "Credential" vs "certificate".** The footer offers *"Search completion certificates"* and *"Credential verification — available once the first credential is issued"* side by side; the account tab is *Certifications*; the document is a *Certificate of Completion*. DR-01 has one credential, so one word for it in user-facing copy.

**F11 · Register form.** Good: four fields, each with *"as on your government ID"*, the consent, the reason for each field. One hint reads oddly — *"One account per email address; this is how you sign in"* under Email. On a phone the Date of birth and Country sit side by side and the country select is narrow. Minor.

## 5. What is good — keep it

- **Empty states** (My Trainings, Orders, Certifications) say what will appear, when, and offer the one next step.
- **Checkout** is a model: *Your date → Your price → Agree and pay*, the price basis explained (*"set by the country on your profile — if that is wrong, update your profile before paying"*), the refund tiers in view, the consent line naming all three documents, and *"card details are never entered on this site"*.
- **Free Learning reading experience**: a calm measure, the book's own emphasis, topic numbering, previous/next, a search that works, self-check under the text with right/wrong/not-answered and explanations.
- **Schedule** filtered by training with *About this training* back-links; the per-date card says format, date, length and seats in one line.
- **Honesty everywhere** — "not yet published", "To be confirmed", "not the Academy's credential", "we do not save your diagnostic results" — the portal never pretends.
- **Dark theme** is complete and legible; the phone menu holds search, navigation and Sign in in one place.
- **Accessibility**: every page in the Playwright suite passes axe WCAG 2.2 AA.

## 6. Decisions for the founder

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **U1** | Fix the four defects in §3 now? | **Yes** — all are GREEN-gate UI changes with tests; about half a day. |
| **U2** | One user-facing word for the product: **training**, **course**, or **programme**? | **Training** (matches the navigation, HRD Corp language and the Certificate of Completion wording); URLs unchanged. |
| **U3** | Home page: keep the full inline diagnostic, or a teaser that opens `/free-learning/diagnostic`? | **Teaser**; shorten the hero to one message; review the promise copy with §3 item 4. |
| **U4** | Price cards: show all four regions (as now) or lead with the visitor's region and fold the rest? | **Lead with the visitor's region**, button CTA "See dates and register". |
| **U5** | Offer the US$10 result document after a **fail**, or only after a pass? | **Only after a pass** (a fail keeps its ID and verify page; the document offer appears once the person passes). If the document should record fails too, keep as is but move the offer below the retake button. |
| **U6** | Adopt the question-stem rule (*no "the chapter"; name the subject*) and re-edit the 100 reviewed questions? | **Yes**; applied to every later batch by the import script's validation. |
