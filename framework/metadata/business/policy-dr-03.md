## Purpose
Records the addition of a second product line, free self-paced learning from the founder's book *I Am Datapedia!*, and a free Knowledge Check, beside expert-led training.

## Description
- **Decision.** The Academy offers two kinds of learning: expert-led training (paid, certified by the Certificate of Completion) and free self-paced learning (the founder's own published work). Free learning is free: no fee, no seat, no order; reading and topic self-checks store nothing about the reader. The Knowledge Check is for signed-in account holders; at approval it had a 70 % pass mark, no time limit and unlimited retakes, and its result is stored.
- **Date approved.** 2026-09-27.
- **Amends.** DR-02 §1 "We are not" (the phrases "a generic LMS" and "a self-paced content consumption platform" no longer exclude the Academy's own free learning content). Everything else in that list stands.
- **Binding effect.** Binding on all three specifications and on DR-02. DR-01 unchanged: a passed Knowledge Check produced a "Knowledge Check result" with a unique verifiable ID, not a credential, not called a certificate. Showing the result document needed a review of the free learning plus a US$10 unlock (Pakistan exempt; non-refundable once shown). Not decided here: object storage, question authorship (10 questions per topic as drafts until reviewed), rights, and the schema (approved separately).
- **Later changes.** DR-04 narrowed §3 naming; DR-05 narrowed §3 for passed results; DR-06 superseded the sizes (50/100/200 questions), the no-time-limit rule and the 70 % mark for new attempts.
- **Plain words.** Alongside paid expert-led training, there is a free reading-and-self-test line. Its results are stored and verifiable but are not the Academy's credential.
