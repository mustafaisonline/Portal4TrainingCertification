## Purpose
Describes the policy on cancelling or moving a training registration, refunds, and what happens if the Academy cancels. This file describes it; it does not reproduce it.

## Description
- **Version identifier.** `2026-10-02` (earlier draft identifiers used a `DRAFT-` prefix). Server consent version set per CR-0610.
- **Publication status.** `published`. Counsel review not determined from the source.
- **Language.** English only.
- **Rule source.** The refund schedule is a founder decision of 2026-09-21 (noted in the source as resolving OQ-2 / OQ-9 for scheduled public offerings); it is stated in the document's table and summarised in Terms section 8. The figures are not repeated here; read the source. A test (`tests/unit/legal-content.test.ts`) checks the numbers are present.
- **Sections (11).** 1 What this policy covers (including "Support the Academy" one-off payments, which have no refund tier); 2 Cancelling your registration; 3 How to cancel; 4 Transferring to a later date instead; 5 If the Academy cancels or reschedules; 6 How and when refunds are paid; 7 Sales and Service Tax on refunds; 8 Private and corporate cohorts; 9 Your rights under consumer law; 10 Changes to this policy; 11 Contact.
- **Related fees outside the schedule.** DR-06 states the certificate unlock fee is non-refundable once shown; DR-08 states the interest fee is outside the cancellation schedule and non-refundable (per those DRs).
