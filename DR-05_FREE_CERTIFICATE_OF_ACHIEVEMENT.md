# DECISION RECORD DR-05 — A PASSED FREE KNOWLEDGE CHECK MAY ISSUE A "CERTIFICATE OF ACHIEVEMENT"

**Status:** **Approved by the founder, 2026-09-29.** The intent was approved on 2026-09-29 (Milestone 15, question Q1: *"it will be free attempt but for certificate, user must pay USD10"*); the founder then read the drafted text and replied *"I am fine with this."* Narrows DR-03 §3 and DR-04 §2.2. DR-01 is **unchanged** (§3). (Applied the same day: the Terms, Privacy notice and refund policy drafts were updated to the "Certificate of Achievement" wording and re-versioned `DRAFT-2026-09-29` — the §4 items about their wording are now done; renewal of an expired free certificate is still not built.)

## 1. Why

DR-03 §3 and DR-04 §2.2 say a Knowledge Check result is never called "a certificate". That rule protects DR-01 — the Academy has **one credential** — from being confused with a free, unproctored, self-graded test. The founder now wants a **passed** free Knowledge Check to produce a printable, verifiable **Certificate of Achievement** (free to take; viewing/downloading the certificate stays behind the existing review + unlock-fee gate, Pakistan exempt, admin-switchable). Read literally, DR-03/DR-04 forbid naming that document, so the rule is narrowed here rather than worked around.

## 2. The decision (narrows DR-03 §3 and DR-04 §2.2)

1. A **passed** Knowledge Check may issue a document titled **"Certificate of Achievement"**. It is shown on the certificate itself, in the result page's document card, and as the *Type* on the public verification page.
2. **A failed result never produces one** and is never called one; it keeps its ID and verification page ("Not passed").
3. The document is **never** called a "Certificate of Completion", never called "the Academy's credential", and never claims an HRD Corp status. It carries the plain disclosure: *"This certificate recognises a passed online Knowledge Check. It is not the Academy's Certificate of Completion, which is earned by attending an expert-led training."* The verification page keeps the sentence that a Knowledge Check is not the Certificate of Completion.
4. It is **valid one year** from the pass date (MYT calendar date), can be **revoked** by a platform administrator (with a recorded reason, audited, permanent), and the verification page says **Valid / Expired / Revoked** in words.
5. It is **system-generated**: no signature, no signature line, no signatory.
6. **Unchanged from DR-04 §2:** "Free Certifications" remains the product line's page/menu name; the free product is still not a second path to the Academy's credential; the account area's "Your Knowledge Check results" list still keeps these results visibly apart from Certificates of Completion; nothing about eligibility, the 70 % pass mark, retakes or the unlock fee changes.
7. The result is still **not added to the credential-integrity policy** as a credential. (Whether that policy should mention the Certificate of Achievement is left for the founder's review — see §4.)

## 3. What DR-01 still means (unchanged)

Nothing here creates a second credential. The Academy issues **one credential**: the Certificate of Completion, earned by attending an expert-led training. The Certificate of Achievement records a passed self-test and says so on its face.

## 4. What this record does NOT decide (for the founder's review)

- Whether the credential-integrity policy, the Terms, the Privacy notice and the refund policy should be updated to mention the Certificate of Achievement (their DRAFT text still speaks of a "result document"). **Not changed by Milestone 15.**
- Whether an *expired* Certificate of Achievement can be renewed for a fee. Today it lapses; the free retake is the route to a new one. No renewal payment is built.
- The unlock order/setting label "Knowledge Check result document" is a stored commercial record and was left as is.
