## Purpose
Records that a passed free Knowledge Check may issue a printable, verifiable document titled "Certificate of Achievement", without creating a second credential.

## Description
- **Decision.** A passed check may issue a document titled "Certificate of Achievement" (on the certificate, the result page's document card and as the Type on the public verification page). A failed result never produces one. The document is never called a Certificate of Completion or the Academy's credential, never claims HRD Corp status, and carries a disclosure that it recognises a passed online Knowledge Check and is not the Certificate of Completion. Valid one year from the pass date (MYT calendar date); revocable by a platform administrator with a recorded, audited reason; the verification page says Valid / Expired / Revoked. System-generated: no signature or signatory. Viewing remains behind the review plus unlock-fee gate (Pakistan exempt, admin-switchable).
- **Date approved.** 2026-09-29 (founder: "it will be free attempt but for certificate, user must pay USD10", then "I am fine with this" on the drafted text).
- **Narrows.** DR-03 §3 and DR-04 §2.2. DR-01 unchanged.
- **Not decided.** Whether the credential-integrity policy, Terms, Privacy and refund policy mention it (the legal drafts were updated the same day); renewal of an expired certificate (not built; a free retake is the route).
- **Plain words.** Pass the free assessment and you can obtain a "Certificate of Achievement" that says plainly it is not the Academy's credential.
