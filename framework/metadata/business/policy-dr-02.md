## Purpose
Records what kind of organisation the Academy is and how it delivers: expert-led training, with the portal supporting rather than being the place where learning happens.

## Description
- **Decision.** The Academy is an independent professional training and certification organisation for data and AI capability. Delivery is expert-led in four first-class forms: face-to-face, live online (real-time interaction; explicitly not watching video online), corporate/private engagements, and international/on-site delivery. The portal supports the ecosystem. Certification relies on participation plus required learning activities plus meaningful assessment plus applied evidence; attendance alone never earns a credential.
- **Date approved.** 2026-08-31 (triggered by the founder's vision clarification of 2026-08-30/31 and the Vision Alignment Audit).
- **Supersedes.** Every prior statement positioning the product as a self-paced course platform, a browsable course catalogue, or a professional association / standards body. Section 12 of the record lists each superseded statement by document and location (MVP Build Spec, Blueprint, Mockup Spec, built mockup).
- **Amendments.** A1 (2026-09-02): the market-facing unit is now called a "course" (word changes, model does not). DR-03 (2026-09-27) narrows §1 "We are not": the Academy's own free self-paced learning is not excluded.
- **Binding effect.** Binding on all three root specifications. It does not change DR-01, the architecture records, or governance (section 13). Confirmed founder decisions (section 10) include: no purely self-paced route to the credential in V1; AI Tutor deferred, not retired; individual and corporate both first-class; small deliberate launch portfolio with no fixed count; design for an expert network without fabricating one; "Academy" retained only as a temporary working name.
- **Plain words.** The business is expert-led training whose outcome is provable. It is not a course marketplace, not a video library and not an association. Open items left undecided by the record: launch inventory, how participation is represented in the credential model, the replacement for differentiator D3, assessor supply, online payment for individuals, the exact corporate workflow boundary, and the final name.
